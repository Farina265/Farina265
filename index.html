Stock In / Adjustment

Goods receive karne, waste, ya manual correction ke liye. Simple PCS system.
Jaise 50 khareedein, 2 free: upar "Kitna Maal Aaya" mein 50 (jo paid hai) aur yahan 2 likhein. Stock 52 hoga, lekin buy rate sirf paid 50 pieces ka rahega. Free pieces ka buy rate zero ginta hai, woh sale rate par bikte hain aur poori sale profit mein jati hai.
KG, gram aur LTR ke liye decimal quantity aur actual sale price per unit enter kar sakte hain.
⚠️ Naya price daalne par item ka cost update ho jayega. Purane bills safe rahenge.
Same item kisi doosri company (jaise Company B) se mehnga/sasta aaye to yeh tick karein, Supplier Name likhein aur upar Naya Purchase Price zaroor daalein. Purana stock (Company A) aur uska cost bilkul waisa hi rahega. Sale rate dono ka same rahega. Bechte waqt pehle purana stock, phir naya lot bikta hai aur profit har lot ke apne cost se banta hai.

Cashier yeh KAR sakta hai:
• Billing (naya bill + reprint) — Discount limit tak
• Khata (payment receive)
• Apna PIN change
